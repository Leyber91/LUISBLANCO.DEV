[REAL] Every number and mark is computed in this page by a network of 784, 64, 64 and 10 neurons trained on MNIST (LeCun, Cortes and Burges). The cells are exact, never sampled. The examples in the strip are MNIST test digits; your own drawing is reduced to 28 by 28 the way MNIST’s were (a 20 pixel box, centred by its centre of mass).
