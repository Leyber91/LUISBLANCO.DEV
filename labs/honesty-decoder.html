HONESTY DECODER / instrumental
THE LAB · L212
doctrine 8 · honesty as infrastructure

Every lab's own claim, checked against its code.

Each lab's hub blurb is split into atomic sub-claims, each checked here in your browser against the deployed files, a running lab state or a published value. The figure is computed from what passed; none is typed in. Luis's own systems are graded first.

full method
sub-claim one checkable property of the blurb: an action, a mechanism, a number or scope, a source. Framing is shown, never scored.
verdict supported: the code does what the phrase says, read literally · partial: a weaker or conditional version · unsupported: it does not. A verdict counts only when a check backs it; author assertions stay out.
figure Σ w·v / Σ w over checked sub-claims, v = 1, 0.5, 0. Low counts partial and unverified as 0, high counts them as 1.
[REAL] computed in this browser: every figure, interval, sensitivity, FActScore cross-check, coverage count and check result, from the rows in registry.js and the deployed lab files. Sourced: the public route's capacity (1 reply per 45 s per IP, measured 2026-11-11) and every published reference named beside its check. History: the asserted figures of earlier versions, read from git.
[ILLUSTRATIVE] the weights (one grader's judgement of each sub-claim's share of the claim, each justified in a sentence), and the example claim in the decode box. The verdict readings are the grader's and can be challenged on this page or contested in the repository.
Rows graded 2026-11-11 in the G1 build pass, for Luis, against the commits the receipt names. Sources: Min et al., FActScore, EMNLP 2023; Bardeen, Press & Teukolsky, ApJ 178, 347 (1972); Misner, Thorne & Wheeler, Gravitation (1973); Borucki et al., Science 340, 587 (2013); NASA GSFC Earth Fact Sheet; Shannon, BSTJ 27 (1948); Coxeter, Regular Polytopes (1973); Wang et al., arXiv 2406.04692 (2024).
