THE LAB · L212
change one thing

Type a divergence. Nine stages weave the world that follows.

A deterministic engine computes the physics, runs a climate model, raises the ground and settles it, then rewrites the record from the change forward. When a model route answers, it writes prose on top, and a validator keeps or rejects every line.

Move the divergence's number across its whole range: the engine weaves the world again at 24 values and marks which consequences switch on, and where.

Pick a divergence with a number (gravity, tilt, sunlight, ice density, day length, CO2, oxygen, sea level, an invention's year) and press "sweep this number" under its dial.
