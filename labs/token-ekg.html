evidence

what each number is, and where it comes from
  • [MEASURED]Chunk times are performance.now() readings in milliseconds since the request was sent, taken when the response reader hands over bytes and before any parsing or drawing. They include the network and the event loop, so on localhost they run a little behind the server. This browser's clock resolution: measuring.
  • [MEASURED]The server column is Ollama's final stream line: eval_count, eval_duration, prompt_eval_count, prompt_eval_cached_count, prompt_eval_duration, load_duration and total_duration, reported in nanoseconds and converted to milliseconds for display. Ollama sends one generated token per chunk and counts one more, the end-of-turn token it does not send.
  • [RECORDED]The 14 recordings and the 60-run dataset were made on 2026-11-11 by record-traces.mjs, which runs the same route adapter as this page, on a laptop with an NVIDIA RTX 3500 Ada Laptop GPU (12 GB) and Ollama 0.40.15. The machine was shared with other work while recording, and runs where that shows say so. Model text is verbatim, mistakes included.
  • [RECORDED]The public reply recording is one call to text.pollinations.ai on 2026-11-11, timed with curl. A reply you time here is timed in your browser, and its token counts are the route's own usage field.
  • [REAL]Tokenizers: o200k_base and cl100k_base from gpt-tokenizer 3.4.0 by Bazyli Brzoska (MIT, github.com/niieani/gpt-tokenizer), pinned on jsDelivr, with one fix this page applies for a byte-order-mark lookup. Qwen3 (Qwen/Qwen3-8B) and Mistral v0.3 (mistralai/Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3) are this site's re-implementation of their Hugging Face tokenizer.json files, Apache-2.0. All four are checked in your browser against ids from tiktoken 0.14.0 and Hugging Face tokenizers 0.22.2 on 61 texts.
  • [REAL]The 23 translations are Article 1 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, copied verbatim from the UDHR in XML project (github.com/eric-muller/udhr, commit 588b3f4, 2026-07-28), which reproduces the OHCHR translations and calls them part of our commons.
  • [REAL]Unequal token counts across languages are documented by Petrov, La Malfa, Torr and Bibi, "Language Model Tokenizers Introduce Unfairness Between Languages" (NeurIPS 2023), and Ahia et al., "Do All Languages Cost the Same?" (EMNLP 2023). Subword byte-pair encoding: Sennrich, Haddow and Birch (ACL 2016).
  • [REAL]RMSSD follows the Task Force of the ESC and NASPE (Circulation 93:1043, 1996). SD1 and SD2 follow Brennan, Palaniswami and Kamen (IEEE Trans. Biomed. Eng. 48:1342, 2001). The periodogram is Lomb (Astrophys. Space Sci. 39:447, 1976) and Scargle (Astrophys. J. 263:835, 1982). Mann-Whitney U and the two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test use their normal and asymptotic approximations.
  • [ILLUSTRATIVE]The spike's shape, the paper grid and the electrocardiogram framing are drawing choices. On the strip only a spike's position (arrival time) and height (the wait before it) carry data.
  • [CHOSEN]Two thresholds are this page's choices, not standards: a stall is a wait over 4 x the stream's median and at least 40 ms; a buffered arrival is a wait under 2 ms.
  • [NOT SHOWN]Prices. No price per token appears because none was checked against a dated price list. Causes the page cannot measure, such as batching inside a hosted server, are not claimed.
formulas and the trace format
first token
t of the first chunk, ms after the request was sent.
wait (interval)
ti - ti-1, ms. Under 2 ms counts as buffered and is left out of the statistics below.
decode rate
unbuffered waits counted, divided by their sum, in tokens per second (one chunk is one token on Ollama). The server's rate is eval_count / eval_duration.
mean, sd, p50, p90, p99
of the unbuffered waits; percentiles by linear interpolation.
RMSSD
sqrt(mean((wi - wi-1)2)), ms.
SD1, SD2
SD12 = var(wi - wi-1) / 2; SD22 = 2 sd(w)2 - SD12.
drift
least-squares slope of the wait against context position (prompt tokens + output position), ms per 1,000 tokens, with R2; stalls and buffered waits left out.
stall share
the sum of stall waits over the time from request to last chunk.
periodogram
Lomb-Scargle power of the unbuffered waits against arrival time, from 1/T to the mean Nyquist frequency N/2T; false-alarm p = 1 - (1 - e-z)N.
A against B
Mann-Whitney U on the two sets of waits (effect = the probability that a wait in A exceeds one in B), and the Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance D.
trace JSON, schema 1
{ id, label, kind 'stream' | 'oneshot', route, model, recorded (ISO time), recorder, chunks [[t_ms, kind 'a' | 't', text], ...], tDone, final (server counters or null), usage (OpenAI usage or null), options, note }.
trace CSV
one row per chunk: index, t_ms, wait_ms, kind, buffered, text.
