what is rising, measured

Rising is a rate. This page prints the arithmetic.

Six public feeds read from your browser. Every score is a count over time or over its own baseline, with a 95% interval. Subjects that surface in several feeds join into one topic, and every scan is kept in this browser so the next scan can check what the last one called rising.

        Did the calls hold?

        A call is one of the top 10 items per source. It is a hit when its gain over the follow-up span beats the median gain of every item seen in both observations. Two naive rankings are scored on the same spans.

        Look up your own terms

        Up to five terms, separated by commas. Each is looked up in Hacker News stories (last 24 h against the 7 days before), Wikipedia daily views (latest day against its same-weekday median) and new GitHub repositories (last 7 days against the 21 before), and drawn on one chart against a baseline of 1.
