THE LAB · L212

TRIVERSE · one humanity, three dimensions

The same people, set apart by when they found the artifact.

TRIVERSE is a hard science fiction series in nine books. Its three human worlds are separate dimensions with one species and one substrate beneath them. They run in step until each finds an artifact, at a different stage of its history, and the timing makes them different. Drag through time, zoom from four billion years to a single week, and compare the three at any moment in their own calendars.

ask the timeline

Answers come only from the events this page publishes. Dates and cross-world questions are worked out by the page’s chronology engine; one model call may then phrase the answer, and is rejected if it cites anything outside the events it was given.

The answer quality is measured on a fixed question set in the repository.

three clocks

The codex converts dates by offset (A.T. = CE + 3537; CE = 75 × Cycle + Year − 4750), so spans are differences of native dates; it converts ages by year length (Terran age = Earth age × 365/400).
