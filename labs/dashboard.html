Visitors are browsers that allowed sharing, told apart by a random id. Sessions are page loads. Opens are a lab's own record of being opened; for a lab that does not record itself yet, the links followed to it from the hub. Visible time adds up, per session, the time the page was in view. Actions are every other event that is not an error. From the hub counts the links followed to a lab by where they sat: a card, the side rail, the welcome plate, or anywhere else on the hub.
