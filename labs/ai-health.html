THE LAB · L212
Educational demonstration of interpretability Every patient on this page is synthetic, generated in your browser from invented distributions. No real patient data is used or collected. This is not a medical device, not medical advice and not validated for any care decision; nothing here should inform the care of a real person.

01 · the ward

A published score and a learned model triage the same twenty beds.

NEWS2 adds points from a chart printed in 2017. The model learned its weights from 3,000 synthetic patients in your browser a few seconds ago. Both fire alerts as the shift runs, and both explain themselves below.

      05 · the hidden world

      The ground truth both systems were guessing at, and the dials that made it.

      This generator made every patient on the page. Its coefficients are invented. The hidden-truth curve in section 03 ranks patients by the true probability each one had, which no model can observe, so it marks the ceiling. Change the world and everything regenerates and refits.
