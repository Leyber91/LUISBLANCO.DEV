03 rate and distortion

Each point is one round trip: the bits the capsule spent, as a share of the source's bits, against how much of the source did not come back. For a probabilistic source and a distortion measure, Shannon's rate-distortion function R(D) gives the least rate that keeps the expected distortion at or below D (Shannon 1959). One conversation is not such a source, so the page draws measured points and makes no claim about the bound.
